# 6 Interpret a lineage share

The lineage label, retained isolates and phenotype definition are part of the quantity. `clonal_share` reports the lineage share of a call based on held-out predictions, with the cost of estimating each lineage mean removed lineage by lineage and the score set against a permuted-label control near zero. The control characterizes the estimator; it does not establish a biological mechanism.

The interval is for the lineage share of the represented lineages: the lineages and their sizes are held fixed and only their isolates are drawn again, from the smoothed distribution of each lineage's calls. It says how precisely the collection determines the share of these lineages; it does not describe lineages, farms, countries or years that were not observed. It never leaves out zero unless the studentized permutation test would reject a share of zero, and its upper end also answers for a share accounted for by one lineage whose calls happen to sit close to the rest. The p-value tests whether the lineages differ at all, by permutation of the lineage labels, within sampling units where they are declared.

A call is cut from an ordering the laboratory did not report, the MIC. The call bounds the lineage share of that ordering without a model for it: the lower end is the least share any arrangement of the latent values allows, a theorem given that every call is on the right side of its cut-off and that the latent value is continuous, and its one-sided 95% lower confidence limit is the share that the lineages of the collection are shown to account for. A MIC read on a panel narrows the same bounds; a coarser reading never narrows them.

The support fraction is the share of retained isolates in lineages with at least two members. The share is computed on those isolates, because a singleton lineage cannot be predicted out of sample; the singletons are set aside and counted, and the share describes the repeated lineages. With fewer than two repeated lineages there is no share (status `unavailable`). Few effective lineages and a dominant lineage also require attention: the share then rests on a few lineages, and the report's lineage-structure table shows how many.

Read the method's status, retained denominator and reasons before the point estimate. Missing limits are not zero limits, and a slightly negative share is an estimate near the permutation null, not an error. Lineage association does not identify transmission, causality, resistance mechanisms or clinical utility. See [methods](../methodology.md) for the distinct targets.
