# Reporting checklist

Analyses performed with amr-clonalshare can be reported with the following items.

1. Software version, configuration file and seed, cited with the version-specific archive.
2. Source of the isolates and of the lineage labels, the typing method and the share of isolates typed under each lineage definition.
3. Outcome definition for each agent: phenotype kind, positive category, breakpoint or cut-off with its source, testing standard and version.
4. For MIC analyses, whether panels were recorded or inferred and for which laboratories, the share of readings at an end well, the units, and the panel and covariate columns that define the strata.
5. Analysed records for each result, with the exclusions reported in the input check.
6. Lineage support for every share, and the status of every result, including results that were not reportable.
7. The kind of each uncertainty statement (a 95% interval for the represented lineages, identification bounds, a one-sided lower confidence limit, bootstrap percentile limits of a decomposition, or an e-value) and the resampling budgets behind it.
8. For comparisons of lineage definitions, all six analyses and the five terms of the difference, with their paired intervals.
9. For stratified runs, the stratifying column, the number of isolates per stratum and the isolates without a level; where sampling units were declared, the unit column and the number of units.
10. The sampling frame of the collection and the populations to which the results are not generalised.
