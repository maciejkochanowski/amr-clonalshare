# Reporting checklist

Analyses performed with amr-clonalshare can be reported with the following items.

1. Software version, configuration file and seed, cited with the version-specific archive.
2. Source of the isolates and of the lineage labels, the typing method and the share of isolates typed under each lineage definition.
3. Outcome definition for each agent: phenotype kind, positive category, breakpoint or cut-off with its source, testing standard and version.
4. For MIC analyses, whether panels were recorded or inferred and for which laboratories, the share of readings at an end well, the units, and the covariates entered as fixed effects with their reference levels.
5. Analysed records for each result, with the exclusions reported in the input check.
6. Lineage coverage for every prediction score, and the status of every result, including results that were not reportable.
7. The kind of each uncertainty statement (confidence interval, bootstrap limits under a stated sampling model or identification bounds) and, for the MIC interval, the number of simulated datasets per candidate.
8. For comparisons of lineage definitions, all four matched-record analyses and the three contrasts, without intervals for the contrasts.
9. For stratified runs, the stratifying column, the number of isolates per stratum and the isolates without a level.
10. The sampling frame of the collection and the populations to which the results are not generalised.
