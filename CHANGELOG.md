# Changelog

## [1.0.0]

First release.

- Share of a binary phenotype accounted for by lineage: the cross-validated skill of lineage means in predicting held-out isolates, with isolates assigned to folds within lineages and the cost of estimating each lineage mean removed lineage by lineage, set against the same score of permuted labels; computed on the isolates of repeated lineages, with singletons set aside and counted and the support reported.
- A 95% interval for the lineage share of the represented lineages (`observed_low`, `observed_high`): a studentized (bootstrap-t) smoothed bootstrap that holds the lineages and their sizes fixed and draws the isolates of every lineage again from its Perks-smoothed empirical distribution, the stratum drawn with the reading, studentized by the infinitesimal-jackknife standard error plus the fold spread and the variance of the share under permuted labels, its quantiles taken no closer to the estimate than those of the studentized shares of the permuted labellings (so that an interval leaving out zero is one the studentized permutation test rejects); its upper end is raised to the largest share one lineage alone can account for within the empirical-likelihood region of its readings.
- A permutation test of the between-lineage sum of squares, with lineage labels exchanged within strata and, where a sampling-unit column is declared (`unit_column`), within units; Benjamini-Yekutieli selection of the agents across the panel.
- MIC reading from dilution intervals: the lineage share of the MIC ordering, with every reading scored by its mid-distribution position within its testing laboratory and declared covariates, from the nonparametric maximum-likelihood distribution of the readings, and estimated, tested and given an interval like the share of a call, the scores recomputed in every bootstrap draw; it is the analogue, for the observed lineages, of the rank intraclass correlation extended to censored readings, and assumes no distribution for the readings.
- Bounds on the lineage share of the latent ordering behind a reading or a call (`latent_order.py`): the smallest and the largest rank intraclass correlation of the latent values that the readings allow (Theorem 1), sharp when each stratum is read on one panel and without a model for the latent values; the lower end by Wolfe's minimum-norm-point algorithm with a certified gap, the upper end certified by branch and bound and exact by dynamic programming for up to twenty lineages; and a one-sided 95% lower confidence limit from a dual certificate (Theorem 2) cross-fitted on random halves of the isolates and averaged over 25 splits used both ways. A call's bounds contain those of any panel it was cut from.
- Held-out e-values per antimicrobial with e-BH selection, and a running product over intakes that may be read after any of them, with a rule for the whole programme of looks (Ville's inequality with a Bonferroni threshold).
- Kitagawa decomposition of a prevalence difference between two collections into composition and within-lineage terms, with bootstrap limits that resample isolates or whole sampling units, a shared-support gate, Benjamini-Yekutieli control within each component family and exact recorded-frame bounds for missing outcomes.
- Matched-record comparison of two lineage definitions (`amr-clonalshare-compare`): six analyses and five terms that sum to their difference, separating record selection, the singletons each definition sets aside and relabelling on the records both can score, with paired bootstrap-t intervals for the terms, records and labels held fixed.
- Runs repeated within the levels of a metadata column (`stratify_by`), and the analyses of a call read within the levels of a metadata column (`phenotype_covariate_column`): each call centred on the prevalence of its level, the score of a reading with one cut point, so that the share, its control, its interval and its test concern the same within-level quantity; lineage labels exchanged within a level, the interval drawing each isolate's level with its call, and the bounds of the call within levels.
- Explicit phenotype declarations (clinical S/I/R, WT/NWT, binary), duplicate policies, exact identifier joins with hints for identifiers that differ only in spaces, leading zeros or case, CSV and `.xlsx` input as a laboratory system exports them (comma, semicolon or tab, decimal commas, `n.d.`-style blanks), long or wide call and MIC tables (`mic_agent_columns`), antimicrobial codes read as the agents they stand for (WHONET and EUCAST abbreviations), the tested range per agent from the configuration or from a preset of the EU harmonised panels (`mic_panel_preset`), a unit column for the MIC table (`mic_unit_column`: rows in a unit other than mg/L, such as zone diameters, set aside and counted; the unit recorded per agent), censoring signs read from an operator column or from the value (a blank sign on the highest well beside a `>` reading is the reading of that well), and a first configuration drafted from the tables (`init`), with the observed concentrations per agent and the shape of each table recognised.
- A canonical JSON record, CSV summaries with the p- and q-value of every row and a conclusion per agent (whether the collection shows lineage structure, with the numbers that decide it), input diagnostics with the recorded range of every agent, Markdown and self-contained HTML reports rendered from the record, set in Latin Modern with charts in TeX Gyre Heros, both embedded, a log of the run (`run.log`) and a manifest of file digests; existing output is protected and `--overwrite` replaces only a results directory. One command with subcommands (`run`, `init`, `check`, `example`, `compare`, `gui`, `doctor`, `version`, `completion`), run-time overrides of the budgets and the level, a dry run that prints the configuration with every default, and the shipped collections run by name with the seed of their published record.
- A local form for a laboratory without a command line (`amr-clonalshare-gui`): the tables chosen in the browser, the columns named from lists with the guesses of `--init`, the same run, record and reports written into a folder the command line repeats from; served on the loopback address only, with a token per session, on the standard library alone; an Examples page that runs the example collections of the repository with one click, any configuration file on the computer as `--config` does, tables pasted from a spreadsheet, the recorded range of every agent with one click to declare it, the manual served on the page, and a conclusion per agent beside the numbers. A Windows folder that runs without any installation (`scripts/build_windows_bundle.py`: the embeddable Python, the pinned stack, the package and a file to double-click), built and checked on a Windows runner and attached to every release.
- A result is a function of the data and the seed: it does not depend on the order of rows or antimicrobials or on the number of antimicrobials beside an agent.
- Tests against independent references and exact rational or forty-digit recomputations, invariances, data mutation, whole-artefact comparisons, every value the numerical modules return on 68 fixtures compared with a committed record, refusal wording, cost laws and mutation testing (`CONTRIBUTING.md`); every number the article quotes checked against the record it comes from (`scripts/reconcile_numbers.py`).
- A confirmatory validation campaign whose studies, designs, replicate counts, seeds and decision rules were committed before it runs (`benchmarks/CONFIRMATORY_PROTOCOL.md`); `benchmarks/confirmatory_verdicts.py` applies the rules and writes the verdicts to `benchmarks/results_confirmatory/`.
